    Woodpecker cycle sets

    Pin a fixed set of your mistake cards and solve it in repeated passes ("cycles") — each pass should get faster and more accurate. Aim for 60–75% first-pass accuracy; space cycles 1–3 days apart.

    Cycle complete

    Play-it-out sparring packs

    Goal-based position training: each scenario has a verified goal (win it or hold the draw) — play it out against an opponent at your level, from both sides eventually. Failed scenarios come back for re-tests after 1, 3 and 7 days.

    Endgame canon curriculum

    The essential theoretical endings in three tiers — every lesson goal is tablebase-verified. Read the technique, then play the position out against the engine: pass a lesson to unlock the next, finish a tier to unlock the next tier. Key positions go into your flashcard deck for spaced review.

    Placement test

    Six tablebase-verified play-outs (two per tier) recommend the tier you should start from.

    Endgame leaks in your games

    Scan your stored games for endgames the tablebase says you should have won or held — and get the lessons that fix them.

    Play Like the Master

    Guess-the-move on classic games: you play the winner's moves from the early middlegame. After each guess the reveal contrasts what the master played, what the engine prefers and what a player at your level would play — with partial credit for moves as good as the master's.
